Questions? Answers.

What is 200.Health, and what do you actually do?
We are a licensed longevity practice in the UAE. You get a consultation with a physician, a lab panel where it is needed, and a protocol that is prescribed, dispensed and delivered to you. Nurses handle anything that has to happen in person, at your home.
Where in the UAE do you operate?
Dubai and Abu Dhabi for home visits and same day delivery. Sharjah and Ajman next day. In-person home care is available across the UAE.
Do I need a prescription before I book?
No. Prescription items are prescribed by our own doctors after a consultation, and only when they are clinically appropriate for you. If a treatment is not right for you, we will tell you and suggest what is.
How qualified are the clinicians and nurses?
Every clinician is licensed by the relevant UAE health authority, and every nurse holds a minimum of three years hospital experience before joining us. Credentials are checked at hiring and reverified annually.
How quickly can someone come to my home?
Most home visits in Dubai and Abu Dhabi are covered within 60 to 90 minutes. Blood draws are usually booked for a fixed morning slot, since fasting markers are more reliable early.
How is a peptide protocol actually decided?
Consultation, then bloods, then a protocol. Your doctor sets each item, the dose in units, the timing and the cycle length, and you receive it as a one page schedule alongside the vials. Nothing is added because it is trending.
How should I store and inject what arrives?
Refrigerated between 2 and 8 degrees, in the original packaging, away from light, never frozen. Subcutaneous injection into fat rather than muscle, with sites rotated each time. Your protocol sheet repeats all of this, and a nurse can walk you through the first dose.
What does it cost, and how do I pay?
Consultations, panels and visits are priced per service, and programmes are billed monthly. You will see the full amount before you confirm, and an itemised invoice is available at any time. Card and Apple Pay are accepted in the app.
Can I claim any of this on insurance?
We do not bill insurers directly. Some services may be partly reimbursable under your plan, so we can issue a detailed invoice for you to submit. Confirm eligibility with your insurer before booking.
Is it safe, and what happens if something goes wrong?
Every visit follows a clinical protocol, and a doctor is reachable through the app at any hour. If anything feels unusual or severe, stop and contact us straight away. For an emergency, call 999.
How is my medical data handled?
Records are encrypted, stored on UAE based infrastructure, and visible only to the clinicians involved in your care. We do not sell data. You can request an export or deletion at any time.
Do I need the app, or can I book on the web?
Either works. The app is where your protocol, dose reminders, results and chat history live, so most patients settle into it after the first order.
