Questions? Answers.

How quickly can a nurse arrive?
In Dubai and Abu Dhabi, most home visits are covered within 60 to 90 minutes. Sharjah and Ajman are usually same day or next morning depending on the slot.
Is a drip safe for me?
That is what the clinical screen decides. We check medication, allergies, kidney history and hydration before anything is set up, and a clinician can decline a drip that is not appropriate.
How long does it take?
Most infusions run 30 to 60 minutes. NAD+ takes longer because it has to be given slowly, and your nurse stays with you for the whole thing.
What is actually in the bag?
Every ingredient and dose is listed on your invoice and in the app. If a drip is marketed on vibes rather than contents, it is not one we offer.
Can I book drips regularly?
Yes, and repeat bookings are still screened each time. For anything ongoing we would rather run bloods first and treat what the results show.
Do you cover hotels and offices?
Yes, as long as there is a private space to sit or lie down for the duration. Tell us the location when you book and we will confirm.
