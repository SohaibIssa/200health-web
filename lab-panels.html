Questions? Answers.

Do I need to fast?
For most panels, yes, ideally 10 to 12 hours with water allowed. We confirm the exact instruction when we book you, because it depends on which markers your panel includes.
Who draws the blood?
A licensed nurse employed by us, with a minimum of three years hospital experience. They arrive with sealed equipment, label the tubes in front of you and take the sample straight to the lab.
How fast are results?
Most panels report within 48 hours of the draw. A few specialist markers take longer, and we tell you upfront which ones those are.
Is the readout included?
Yes. Every panel includes a physician readout, because a result you cannot interpret is not useful. You keep the full report either way.
Can I use my own lab results?
Send them over. If they are recent and from an accredited lab, a clinician will use them and tell you what still needs testing rather than repeating the whole panel.
Will insurance cover it?
We do not bill insurers directly, but we can issue a detailed invoice for you to submit. Coverage depends entirely on your plan, so confirm with your insurer before booking.
