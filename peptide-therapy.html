Questions? Answers.

Do I need bloodwork before starting?
Yes. We do not prescribe a peptide protocol without a baseline panel, because dosing and selection depend on what your markers show. If you have recent results from an accredited lab, send them over and a clinician will tell you what still needs testing.
Is peptide therapy legal in the UAE?
Prescription peptides are handled like any other prescription medicine here. They are prescribed by a licensed physician, dispensed by a licensed pharmacy and delivered to you with documentation. We do not supply anything without a prescription.
How is my dose decided?
Your physician sets each item by unit, along with timing and cycle length, based on your bloods, weight, goals and anything else you are taking. Your protocol sheet states all of it in plain language.
How should I store what arrives?
Refrigerated between 2 and 8 degrees, in the original packaging, away from light, never frozen. Let a vial reach room temperature before injecting, and rotate injection sites each time.
How soon will I notice anything?
It depends entirely on the protocol and what you are treating. Recovery protocols often show up first, over several weeks. We set a review point at four weeks so the conversation is based on how you feel and what the markers say.
Can I stay on a protocol indefinitely?
No, and we will not renew one on autopilot. Most protocols cycle, with defined on and off weeks, and every renewal is reviewed by a clinician who can stop or change anything.
