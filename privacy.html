Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

200.Health Medical LLC and 200.Health Clinic LLC ("200.Health", "we", "us") respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, mobile app, and clinical services in the United Arab Emirates, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, phone, email, emirate, preferred appointment times).
  • Health and clinical information needed to deliver care, including consultation notes, prescriptions, lab results and treatment history.
  • App and website usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location (city/emirate).
  • Communications with our clinical and support teams, including WhatsApp messages where you contact us.

How we use your information

  • To provide medical consultations, home visits, lab testing, pharmacy dispensing and follow-up care.
  • To confirm appointments, send clinical updates and, where you opt in, service-related messages.
  • To meet regulatory, licensing and record-keeping obligations under UAE health authorities.
  • To improve our services, app experience and website.

Legal basis and health data

Health information is processed only for legitimate clinical purposes, with your consent where required, and in line with applicable UAE health and data protection regulations. Clinical decisions are made solely by licensed healthcare professionals.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with licensed laboratories, pharmacies, nurses and clinicians involved in your care; technology providers that host our systems under strict confidentiality; and regulators or authorities when required by law.

Retention and security

We retain records for as long as needed to provide care and meet legal requirements. We use encryption, access controls and staff training to protect data in transit and at rest.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data where applicable law allows. Contact us using the details below. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP or emailing us.

International transfers

Where data is processed outside the UAE, we ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.
