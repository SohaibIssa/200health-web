200.Health provides consultations, laboratory panels, home nursing visits, infusion therapy, prescription treatments and wellness programmes through its licensed clinical entities and licensed partners in the United Arab Emirates. Pricing varies by service, emirate, provider and time of booking. Programmes are billed on a recurring basis and cover a defined set of services at a bundled rate, while single services are charged when the booking is confirmed. A full itemisation of fees is available on request at any time.

Care is delivered by licensed doctors, nurses and pharmacists through 200.Health's licensed clinical entities, or through independently licensed providers accessed via the platform. All clinical decisions and medical opinions are made solely by the treating licensed professional. Health content published here is for general information only and is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation. Peptide and prescription treatments are dispensed only where a treating clinician judges them clinically appropriate, and never on request alone. For anything specific to your health, symptoms or history, speak with our clinical team or your own physician.

200.Health does not bill insurance providers directly and does not guarantee coverage. Some services may qualify for full or partial reimbursement under your private plan, subject entirely to your insurer's terms. A detailed invoice can be issued for you to submit. VAT applicability varies by service category and follows the current regulations of the UAE Federal Tax Authority, so please refer to your invoice for a complete breakdown.

By providing your phone number you agree to receive communications from 200.Health, including appointment confirmations, delivery updates, dose reminders and, where applicable, promotional messages. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP or by contacting support. Personal and health related data is collected, stored and processed in line with applicable UAE data protection law. See the privacy policy for details.
