How the programme runs.

No queue, no walk in clinic. A consultation, bloods, then a prescription only if a doctor judges it right for you.

Step 01 · Consultation

A doctor decides, not a form.

History, medication, previous attempts and your goal. If a GLP-1 is not appropriate, we will tell you and offer the alternative.

Step 02 · Baseline

Bloods before the first dose.

Metabolic and thyroid markers, kidney and liver function, drawn at home so we know you are safe to start.

Step 03 · Every two weeks

Reviewed, titrated, delivered.

A short review each fortnight, dose adjusted where needed, and the next pen delivered cold before you run out.

Questions? Answers.

Will I definitely be prescribed a GLP-1?
No. It is prescribed only where a physician judges it clinically appropriate after your consultation and bloods. If it is not right for you, we say so and offer the metabolic reset instead.
Which medications do you use?
Your doctor selects from the GLP-1 medications licensed and available in the UAE, based on your history, tolerance and what is in stock. They will explain the choice, the titration plan and the alternatives before you start.
What does it cost?
The programme is billed monthly and covers the consultation, fortnightly reviews, bloods at the intervals we schedule and delivery. Medication is invoiced separately at pharmacy price, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.
What about side effects?
Nausea, reflux and changes in appetite are common early and usually settle as the dose is titrated. Message the clinical team when something feels off. Anything severe means stopping and speaking to a doctor straight away.
Do I have to inject myself?
Yes, and it is a small subcutaneous injection you can do in seconds. A nurse can walk you through the first one at home or over video if you would rather not do it alone.
What happens when I stop?
We plan for it. There is a taper and a maintenance phase, with reviews continuing while you come off, because stopping abruptly is where most of the weight comes back.
